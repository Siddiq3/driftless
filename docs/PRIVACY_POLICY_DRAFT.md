# Driftless Privacy Policy — DRAFT (not legal advice; needs review before publishing)

_Last updated: [date of publication]_

Driftless helps you understand and reduce time spent scrolling short videos. We designed it to keep
your data on your phone.

## What stays on your phone
- **App minutes** for Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, Snapchat, Moj and ShareChat, per day. Candidate
  **video counts** are only configured for Instagram and YouTube and remain accuracy-unvalidated.
- **How counting works:** if you turn on the Driftless accessibility service, it runs only inside
  Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, Snapchat, Moj and ShareChat. Video player structures are inspected
  only in Instagram and YouTube, where candidate rules exist. Labels are turned into an unreadable
  code immediately and are never saved or sent anywhere.
- **Usage Access** (if you allow it) gives Driftless the minutes you spend in those six apps.
- **Goals, streaks and Wick's progress.**
- Raw counting events are deleted after 30 days. Daily totals stay until you delete them.

We never read your messages, never take screenshots, never record what videos you watched, and never
act inside other apps for you.

## What we keep if you create an account
- Your email address (to sign in), a handle and optional display name.
- If you turn on **Cloud backup**: daily totals (minutes, and video counts only once their accuracy is
  confirmed), your goals, and a random install id. Kept for up to 400 days.
- If you turn on **Share with friends**: friends in a challenge with you can see your score for that
  challenge. If it's off, you are never shown.
- If you subscribe to **Pro**: Google Play tells us whether your subscription is active. We never see
  your payment details.
- If you allow notifications: a push token to deliver them (removed after 180 days of inactivity).

## Who we share with
No one, except: Amazon Web Services (hosting), Google Play (subscriptions) and Firebase (delivering
notifications), acting on our behalf. We do not sell data and use no advertising or analytics SDKs.

## Your choices
- Turn counting off any time in Settings › Accessibility or Usage Access.
- **Download your data** and **Delete your account** in the app (Privacy & data). Deletion removes
  your account and cloud data immediately; encrypted backups expire within 35 days.
- Delete everything stored on your phone from the same screen, or by uninstalling.

## Children
Driftless is for adults (18+). We don't knowingly collect data from children.
