# Delete your ScrollSnooze account

Draft for a public HTTPS webpage. Developer: Siddiq Kolimi.

## In the app

Sign in to the account you want to delete. Open **Privacy & data → Delete my account**
and confirm the request. If an interrupted deletion appears, resume it. Recent Google
sign-in may be needed. This deletes your ScrollSnooze account, not your Google account.

## Without the app

Email **siddiqkolimi.dev@gmail.com** with the subject **ScrollSnooze account deletion**.
Include the Google account email used to sign in and your ScrollSnooze display name or
handle if known. Do not include your password, verification codes, ID tokens or private
viewing history. We will verify account ownership before processing the request; knowing
an email address alone does not authorize deletion.

## What is removed

Account profile, backed-up usage history, saved cloud goals, device registrations, friend
relationships and challenge participation are handled by the account deletion workflow.
On-device data is separate: use the local reset control in Privacy & data, or uninstall
the app. Uninstalling alone does not request deletion of cloud account data.

and any required legal/security exceptions. Replace this draft note with verified timings.
Publish it at a stable public URL and provide that URL in Play Console's Data safety form.
