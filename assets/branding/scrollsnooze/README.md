# ScrollSnooze identity — concept v1

Created with the built-in image_gen tool. The active [purple identity](purple/README.md) is connected to the app, launcher, splash and native overlays. The peach logo and character set below are retained as earlier design sources.

- `mascot-v1.png`: transparent full-body peach brain mascot, with a distinct S-shaped crown curl, sleepy eyes and a small confident smile.
- `logo-v1.png`: transparent horizontal head-emblem and ScrollSnooze wordmark, designed for dark app surfaces.
- `icon-mark-v1.png`: transparent standalone head emblem. This is source artwork; launcher-specific padding, backgrounds and native icon sizes still need to be generated when the identity is adopted.

## Palette

Apricot `#FFD09D`, coral `#FFAE94`, cream `#FFF9ED`, cocoa `#492B16`.

## Final prompts

### Standalone icon emblem

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: standalone square app-icon emblem master with genuine transparent background.
Reference image role: the attached ScrollSnooze horizontal logo is the exact identity reference. Extract and redraw ONLY its brain-head emblem as a standalone clean icon. Keep the same five-lobed peach brain-cloud silhouette, recognizable S-shaped crown fold, relaxed half-lidded eyes, raised eyebrow and cheeky smile. Preserve the visual identity, colors and proportions. Remove ALL wordmark lettering.
Primary request: One original finished ScrollSnooze icon mark, a compact sleepy smiling brain head with an S-shaped fold. Premium clean graphic illustration, bold dark cocoa outlines #492B16, warm apricot #FFD09D with coral #FFAE94 and cream #FFF9ED. Simplify small interior details for readability at 32 pixels; keep expressive eyelids and S curl. Broad flat fills with only very subtle shading. Crisp outlines and professionally balanced silhouette.
Composition: square canvas, exactly one head centered, symbol occupies about 74 percent of canvas width and height, generous even transparent padding for adaptive launcher masks. Head-only, no body, no hands. Front facing, level head without the tilt. Fully within frame.
Background: transparent alpha, no app icon tile or rounded square, no scene, no checkerboard image.
Constraints: absolutely no text, no extra characters, no Zs, no phone, no stars, no watermarks, no shadows outside the silhouette. This is the same character as the reference logo, not a new character.


### Mascot

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: original full-body mobile app mascot master, isolated transparent PNG.
Primary request: Design one polished, memorable original mascot for ScrollSnooze, a playful app that helps young adults reduce endless Reels and Shorts scrolling. The mascot should be an endearing little sleepy brain creature: a friendly accountability companion, internet-savvy and quietly cheeky, not a baby toy and not a scary anatomical brain.
Subject: oversized soft rounded brain-cloud head, five or six broad sculpted lobes rather than realistic wrinkles, an immediately recognizable single curled S-shaped fold at the crown, tiny pear-shaped body, two stubby feet, short rounded arms. Warm apricot peach #FFD09D and soft coral #FFAE94, cream highlights #FFF9ED, dark cocoa facial details #492B16. Large half-lidded dark cocoa eyes, expressive thick upper lids, one eyebrow gently raised, a tiny asymmetric confident smile, subtle warm cheeks. Relaxed front three-quarter pose, one hand raised in a small friendly wave. Both feet clearly visible. Simple expressive face with space for future fresh, focused, tired and drained variants.
Style/medium: premium character illustration with soft tactile clay-like 3D volume, smooth matte finish, restrained shading, crisp readable silhouette, refined shapes, clean studio lighting. Distinctive charming proportions, editorial app-mascot quality, not generic emoji. Broad sculpting instead of noise. Head and body feel like the same creature.
Composition: one character only, centered full body, upright, fills around 80 percent of square canvas with generous clear padding on every side. No cropping.
Scene/backdrop: genuinely transparent alpha background. No floor, no scenery, no opaque background, no checkerboard drawn into the image, no cast shadow outside the character.
Constraints: completely original artwork. No existing app branding, no goblin ears, no hats, no nightcap, no bed, no phone, no letters, no wordmark, no floating Z symbols, no watermark, no collage, no additional characters. Keep the head motif simple enough to later simplify into an app icon.

### Logo

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: finished horizontal logo lockup for the mobile app ScrollSnooze, transparent background.
Reference image role: the attached image is the original ScrollSnooze mascot identity reference, NOT a layout to reproduce. Preserve its recognizable warm peach brain-cloud head, S-shaped crown curl, sleepy friendly face and cheeky little smile. Simplify these into a clean compact graphic symbol.
Primary request: Design one cohesive professional logo consisting of a simplified mascot HEAD emblem to the left and the exact wordmark "ScrollSnooze" to the right on ONE line. The emblem and wordmark should feel custom designed together. Single lockup only, not a presentation board.
Logo emblem: confident flat rounded shapes, warm apricot #FFD09D with muted coral #FFAE94 accents, thick consistent dark cocoa #492B16 outlines and facial details. Compact five-lobed silhouette, one clear large S-shaped curl incorporated into the crown, half-lidded eyes and small smiling mouth. No body or hands. Very few interior lines, no anatomical fine wrinkles. Icon readable at small mobile header sizes, no detailed clay texture.
Wordmark (verbatim): "ScrollSnooze" with capital S in Scroll and capital S in Snooze. Bold bespoke rounded sans lettering, close but balanced spacing, softly squarish organic letter shapes, friendly and confident not childish. Scroll in warm cream #FFF9ED and Snooze in apricot #FFD09D, dark cocoa outline around all letters so the logo remains legible on cream and nearly black backgrounds. Strong typographic craft, accurate spelling, clean kerning, no script fonts, no narrow thin letters, no letters substituted with illustrations.
Composition: horizontal landscape canvas, compact balanced head emblem at left, generous consistent gap then wordmark. All elements on one optical centerline. Lockup fills about 85 percent of canvas width with generous transparent margins and no cropping. Do not make a square collage.
Style: polished flat vector-like branding with crisp clean edges. No 3D, no photographic texture, no gradients, no bevels, no drop shadows.
Background: genuine transparent alpha. No rectangle behind logo, no checkerboard pattern.
Constraints: one head emblem and one wordmark only. No tagline, no mockup, no phone, no stationery, no extra icons, no floating sleep symbols, no watermark, no other brand names. Original ScrollSnooze identity, no reuse of another app logo.
