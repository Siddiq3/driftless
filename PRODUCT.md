# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

iPhone is planned later (Screen Time based); design should not block adapting to it, but Android is the target today.

## Users

Young adults in general — students and young working people — who lose study, work and sleep time to Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts and want to scroll less without quitting the apps. Setup asks whether they are a school student, college student, working, or something else. Early users and content lean Telugu, but the audience is not limited to India or one language.

## Product Purpose

ScrollSnooze counts individual Reels and Shorts as they are watched, lets people set a daily limit, reminds them as they approach it (50/80/90/100%), and can pause the feed at the limit or during focus times they choose. Success is a person watching fewer short videos day to day while still feeling the app is on their side.

## Positioning

It counts videos, not just minutes: a live count bubble over the Reels/Shorts feed, limits in reels per day, and pauses that cover only the short-video feed (or the whole app, if chosen). A purple brain companion reacts to the day, grows with kept days (streaks), and interrupts long runs with meme breaks in the person's chosen language.

## Operating Context

- Used on Android phones alongside Instagram and YouTube; the feed pause and count bubble draw over those apps via an Accessibility service the person must enable.
- Focus & app pauses: pick apps, a daily limit, focus times (presets or custom, overnight allowed), what to pause (Reels & Shorts or whole app), feed breaks; a master switch turns pauses on.
- Home screen widget, Progress (streaks, achievements, Wrapped), Friends daily leaderboard, Daily goals.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Counting is experimental: no Instagram or YouTube version is confirmed accurate yet, so counts are labelled experimental.
- Google Play Accessibility policy is strict; on-screen text is read only on Reels/Shorts players, never saved, and only for counting. The app was rejected once over Accessibility disclosure — any new use of screen content needs disclosure changes first.
- All features are free; there are no subscriptions.
- UI copy is English; meme captions follow a chosen meme language (English, Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam).
- Back and Home always work while a pause is showing.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: ScrollSnooze. Mascot: the purple brain companion (formerly "Wick" in code; that name is retired from user-facing copy).
- Voice: playful buddy, never shaming. It jokes ("Log off. Wake up."), and a bad day makes the companion sleepy, never dead or punished.

## Evidence on Hand

- No testimonials, ratings, user counts or press. Do not invent any.
- Meme GIFs are third-party Tenor clips of real people; three are pending rights clearance (`docs/MEME-ASSETS.json`).

## Product Principles

1. On the person's side: nudge with humour and rewards, never guilt.
2. Honest numbers: say when a count is experimental or unknown; never claim accuracy we don't have.
3. Control stays with the person: limits and pauses are their own choices, easy to change, and Back/Home always escape.
4. Privacy first: screen text is processed in memory and never stored or uploaded.
